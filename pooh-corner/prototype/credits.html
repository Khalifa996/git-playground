About this prototype

An independent design concept

This is a clickable concept for a redesign of the Pooh Corner websites, made for discussion with the business. It is not the official website, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pooh Corner.

What's real and what's simulated

ElementStatus
Opening hours, parking, travel, tearoom, afternoon tea, accessibility and FAQ detailsTaken from poohcorner.co.uk on 23 October 2026. To be confirmed with the business before any use.
Product names, prices, descriptions and images27 products taken from shop.poohcorner.co.uk on 24 October 2026. Images are loaded from the shop's own image server and belong to their owners.
Delivery informationFrom the current shop's product pages. To be confirmed.
Basket, checkout and newsletter sign-upSimulated. No orders are placed and no data is sent. The basket is stored only in this browser.
Search and filtersWorking, but only across the sample products. The live build would use Shopify's search and filters across the full catalogue.
"Open now" statusCalculated in the browser from the published hours. Bank holidays aren't detected.
Tearoom and museum imagesPlaceholders, deliberately. These need the business's own photography.
